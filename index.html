🎗️ Fentanyl Test Strip Dilution Calculator (DanceSafe Standard) Prevent False Positives

Why dilution matters: MDMA and methamphetamine cause false positives on fentanyl strips if the water is too concentrated. Follow these exact ratios:

MDMA, 2C-B, or Amphetamines 10 mg powder in 5 mL water (1 tsp)

Dip up to wavy line for 15s, lay flat for 3 mins. 2 red lines = Negative (even if one line is very faint). 1 red line = DANGER (Positive for Fentanyl).

Ketamine, Cocaine, or Tuci 10 mg powder in 1 mL water

After testing, you can still consume or evaporate the clean water solution so zero material is wasted.

🛡️ High-Stakes Full-Dose Contraindication Reference
⛔ Lithium + Classic Psychedelics

Extreme risk of grand mal seizures and acute fugue states. (Note: Lamictal/Lamotrigine does NOT share this seizure risk).

⛔ MAOIs / Ayahuasca + MDMA / SSRIs / 5-MeO

Blocks monoamine breakdown; triggers fatal serotonin syndrome and malignant hyperthermia.

⛔ Ketamine + Alcohol / Opioids / GHB

Dual CNS depression causes profound ataxia, blackout, and lethal aspiration from vomiting while unconscious.

⚠️ Tramadol & DXM Cough Meds

Lower seizure threshold and inhibit serotonin reuptake; strictly contraindicated with psychedelics and entactogens.

1. Create a Safe Container

Dim harsh lights, lower room temperature if warm, silence phones, and never crowd or hover over the journeyer.

2. Sitting, Not Guiding

Don't steer their thoughts or analyze what they see. Speak slowly, validate feelings ("You are safe, take your time"), and ask before any physical touch.

3. Talk Through, Not Down

Remind them: "If you see a closed door, open it; if you feel a wave, breathe and float with it." Fighting the current creates panic.

4. Change the Sensory Channel

If stuck in a thought loop, gently change one thing: move to the floor ("Flude grounding"), sip cool water, put on a warm blanket, or switch to soft rain audio.
